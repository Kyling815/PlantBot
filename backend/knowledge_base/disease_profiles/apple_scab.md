# Apple — Scab (*Venturia inaequalis*)

## Overview
Apple scab is the most economically important fungal disease of apple trees worldwide, caused by *Venturia inaequalis*. It affects both leaves and fruit.

## Symptoms
- **Olive-green to brown velvety lesions** on leaves and fruit
- Lesions on leaves may cause curling, yellowing, and premature drop
- Fruit lesions: dark, scabby, corky patches; fruits may crack or be deformed
- Severe infections can defoliate the entire tree by midsummer

## Causal Agent
- **Pathogen**: *Venturia inaequalis* (fungus — Ascomycete)
- **Type**: Leaf and fruit scab
- **Spread**: Ascospores from overwintering leaf debris; conidia during the growing season

## Conditions Favoring Disease
- Cool, wet spring weather (13–24°C)
- Extended leaf wetness (Mills periods)
- Dense orchard canopy with poor air movement

## Disease Cycle
1. Fungus overwinters as pseudothecia in fallen leaves
2. Ascospores discharged during spring rains (primary infection)
3. Conidia produced on lesions cause secondary infections throughout summer
4. Disease pressure highest during wet springs
