# Bacterial Disease Control Guide

## Overview
Bacterial plant diseases are caused by pathogenic bacteria and are often favoured by warm, wet conditions. Unlike fungal diseases, they cannot be cured with fungicides.

## Recommended Treatments

| Product | Active Ingredient | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Copper-based bactericide | Copper hydroxide / oxychloride | Spray every 7–10 days |
| Streptomycin (where legal) | Streptomycin sulfate | Only in severe outbreaks |
| Kasugamycin | Kasumin | Foliar spray |

## Application Guidelines
1. **Act immediately** — Bacterial diseases spread very quickly
2. **Remove infected material** — Cut and bag infected leaves/stems; do not compost
3. **Disinfect tools** — Use 10% bleach solution between cuts
4. **Apply copper bactericide** — Provides protective barrier on leaf surfaces
5. **Avoid wetting foliage** — Switch to drip irrigation

## Cultural Controls
- Source certified disease-free seeds and transplants
- Avoid working in the field when plants are wet
- Practice crop rotation (3–4 year cycle)
- Maintain plant vigour through balanced nutrition

## Important Notes
- Copper products can be phytotoxic if over-applied — follow label instructions
- Streptomycin use is restricted or banned in many countries due to resistance risk
- Remove severely infected plants from the field entirely
