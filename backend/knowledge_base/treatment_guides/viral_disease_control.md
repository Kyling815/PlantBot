# Viral Disease Control Guide

## Overview
Plant viruses cannot be cured once a plant is infected. Management focuses on **prevention**, **vector control**, and **removal of infected material**.

## Common Viral Diseases in PlantBot
- Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV)
- Tomato Mosaic Virus (ToMV)
- Cucumber Mosaic Virus (CMV)

## Immediate Actions
1. **Remove and destroy** infected plants immediately — do not compost
2. **Disinfect hands and tools** after handling infected material
3. **Control insect vectors** (especially whiteflies and aphids) — they spread viruses

## Vector Control
| Vector | Control Method |
|---|---|
| Whiteflies | Yellow sticky traps, imidacloprid (systemic insecticide), reflective mulches |
| Aphids | Insecticidal soap, neem oil, pyrethrin sprays |
| Thrips | Spinosad spray, blue sticky traps |

## Protective Measures
- Use **insect-proof mesh** on seedling beds and greenhouses
- Plant **virus-resistant varieties** where available
- Eliminate weed hosts around the growing area
- Apply **mineral oil** sprays to reduce virus transmission by aphids

## There Is No Cure
Once infected:
- The plant will not recover
- It will continue to be a source of infection for neighbouring plants
- **Prompt removal is the most effective action**
