# Fungal Disease Control Guide

## Recommended Fungicides

| Active Ingredient | Trade Names | Target Diseases | Application Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mancozeb | Dithane, Penncozeb | Early blight, late blight, scab | 2 kg/ha |
| Chlorothalonil | Bravo, Daconil | Early blight, leaf mold, septoria | 1.5–2 L/ha |
| Azoxystrobin | Amistar, Quadris | Powdery mildew, early blight | 1 L/ha |
| Metalaxyl | Ridomil | Late blight, downy mildew | 2.5 kg/ha |
| Copper hydroxide | Kocide | Many fungal/bacterial diseases | 2–3 kg/ha |

## Application Guidelines

1. **Start early** — Apply at first sign of disease or preventively before wet periods
2. **Rotate fungicides** — Alternate between chemical classes to prevent resistance
3. **Coverage** — Ensure thorough coverage of upper and lower leaf surfaces
4. **Timing** — Apply in early morning or late evening; avoid applying in rain or strong wind
5. **Frequency** — Every 7–14 days during high-risk periods; every 14–21 days otherwise

## Cultural Controls
- Remove and destroy (burn or bury) all infected plant material
- Avoid overhead irrigation; use drip irrigation where possible
- Improve air circulation by pruning and correct plant spacing
- Do not work with plants when foliage is wet (spreads spores)
- Sanitise tools between plants with 70% alcohol or 10% bleach

## Organic Options
- **Copper-based sprays** (copper sulfate, Bordeaux mixture)
- **Baking soda** (sodium bicarbonate) 1% solution — mild suppression
- **Neem oil** — broad-spectrum fungal suppression
- **Compost tea** — improves beneficial microorganism populations
